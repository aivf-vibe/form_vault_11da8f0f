About Aravind Eye Hospital

Pioneering eye care excellence since 1976

Our Mission

Founded by Dr. Govindappa Venkataswamy in 1976, Aravind Eye Hospital has grown from an 11-bed facility in Madurai to India's largest eye care network. Our mission is to eradicate needless blindness through high-quality, affordable eye care services.

Our Vision

To eliminate cataract-related blindness in India and provide world-class eye care services accessible to all, regardless of economic status. We follow a unique model where paying patients subsidize free care for the underprivileged.

Award-Winning Care

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services and procedures

What makes Aravind Eye Hospital unique?

Aravind follows a unique model where paying patients subsidize free care for the underprivileged. We have one of the lowest infection rates globally and have performed over 4 million surgeries with exceptional outcomes.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment by calling +91 452 435 6100, visiting our website, or walking into any of our hospital locations. We also conduct regular eye camps in rural areas.

Do you provide free treatment?

Yes, we provide free or heavily subsidized treatment to over 60% of our patients. Our cross-subsidization model ensures that quality eye care is accessible to all economic segments.

What is the success rate of cataract surgery at Aravind?

Our cataract surgery success rate is over 95% with an infection rate of only 4 per 10,000 surgeries, which is significantly lower than international standards of 6 per 10,000.

Do you have eye banking facilities?

Yes, we have four eye banks located in Madurai, Coimbatore, Tirunelveli, and Puducherry, providing corneal transplantation services across our network.
